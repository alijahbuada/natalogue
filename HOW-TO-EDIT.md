# How to update natalogue

Hi Nat! You update your site with an editor made for it: forms, buttons, and a writing box. No code, and you can't break anything. Every change is saved in history and can be undone.

## Getting in

1. Check your email for an invite from **Pages CMS**.
2. Go to **[app.pagescms.org](https://app.pagescms.org)** and sign in with that email address. It sends you a sign-in link, so there's no password to remember.
3. Open **natalogue**.

Bookmark app.pagescms.org. That's your door to the site, on your laptop or your phone.

**After any save, wait about a minute, then refresh your site to see it.**

---

## Write a new entry

1. Click **Entries** in the sidebar, then the button to add a new entry.
2. Fill in:
   - **Title**: this also becomes the web address, like `/entries/learning-to-rest/`.
   - **Date**: entries are numbered as rows (row 1, row 2…) in date order.
   - **One-line summary** and **Listening to**: optional.
   - **Writing**: your entry. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, italics, lists, quotes, and links. You can add photos too.
3. Click **Save**.

To edit an entry later, click it in the Entries list. To delete one, open it and use the delete option.

**Small touches:**
- A heading becomes pixel type.
- A quote gets a pink crayon edge.
- A horizontal line becomes a little crochet chain.

## Update your shelves

Click **Shelves**. There are two lists:

- **Currently consuming**: what you're in the middle of. The first three also show on the homepage.
- **Favorites**: the keepers.

Each item has a **title**, a **type** (picks the pixel icon), and optional **by**, **how far along**, and **note** fields. You can add, remove, and reorder items. Click an item to open it.

When you finish something, delete it from Currently and add it to Favorites if it earned a spot.

## Change your about page

Click **About page**, write over the sample text, and **Save**. Delete the italic note at the top when you're done.

## Site settings

Click **Site settings** to change:

- **Tagline**: the line under the big title on the homepage.
- **Lace words**: the words in the lace trim at the bottom of every page.
- **Spotify playlist link**: in Spotify, open your playlist, then **Share → Copy link to playlist**, and paste it here. The listening page updates itself whenever you add songs in Spotify.
- **Links elsewhere**: your Instagram, Goodreads, and anything else you add.
- **Show the gallery in the menu**: turn this on once you've added some pieces.

## The art gallery

1. Click **Gallery**, then add an item.
2. Upload a **photo** (square-ish photos look best), give it a **title**, and optionally a **note**.
3. Fill in **photo description** with what's in the picture. It helps people using screen readers.
4. **Save.** When you have a few pieces up, go to **Site settings** and turn on **Show the gallery in the menu**.

---

## If something looks off

- **Your change isn't showing yet?** Give it a minute and refresh. Sometimes it takes two.
- **Something looks broken?** Text Alijah. Nothing is ever lost, and any change can be rolled back.
