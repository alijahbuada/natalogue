i am completely, utterly, pathetically stuck within myself.

my inaction, my useless habits trapped in *my evil phone* … it’s a siren song blaring through my early 20’s! 

the thing is that excuses are my bed and it has dirty laundry all over like i’ve forgotten i still have to lay in it.

i am 23 and for my whole life i’ve been pointing all of my 10 fingers elsewhere when it comes to the inevitable “what’s wrong with me?”.

i think my little ol’ prefrontal cortex is calling to me to finally take action in my own life. because as tired as i am of the career swamp i’m in, i’m even more tired of just watching my own life bolt past me like a train. 

as i work through my 23rd year on this earth, i NEED to practice mindfulness. it’s not some bucket list item or a cute little goal, i NEED to.

why do i feel like i NEED to you may wonder?



because my brain and emotions are insanely unregulated.   


by being mindful, by being as *totally* and *completely aware* of myself as i can be … that’s my key to *actually living* in my own life. not in photographs referencing moments in time i can’t recall, not sweating and stumbling through every unscripted interaction with a stranger … but to be present. to be in my own skin, ***my own natalihn skin***, and be okay with it … :)

now i’m off to consume my mindfulness lessons the only way my dopamine receptors will want to … through the guidance of winnie the pooh <3
