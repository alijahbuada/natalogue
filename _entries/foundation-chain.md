In crochet, the foundation chain is the first row you make. Everything after it gets worked into it. If the chain is too tight, or you lose count, you don't always notice right away. You notice ten rows later, when the edge starts pulling.

That's roughly where I am. I'm building a lot of new things, and I can feel some of the old rows pulling.
